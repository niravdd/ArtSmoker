WHY ONLY A SUBSET IS VENDORED — LICENSE SPLIT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MVPainter's repo is TWO separable halves with DIFFERENT licenses:

  1. Multi-view DIFFUSION (Apache-2.0, commercial-safe) — the actual MVPainter
     contribution. SDXL multi-view UNet + Union ControlNet geometry conditioning.
     This is what we vendor.

  2. The Stage-3 BAKE (infer_paint.py, mvpainter/bake_pipeline*.py,
     mvpainter/custom_rasterizer/, mvpainter/differentiable_renderer/,
     mvpainter/utils.py, scripts/blender_*.py) — vendored Tencent Hunyuan3D code
     under the TENCENT HUNYUAN NON-COMMERCIAL LICENSE, plus bpy (GPLv3).
     *** NOT COMMERCIAL-SAFE — DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED. ***

We use MVPainter's commercial-safe diffusion ONLY, and replace its Stage-3 bake
with ArtSmoker's existing nvdiffrast back-projection baker (the same path used
by the MV-Adapter backend). MVPainter's diffusion emits 6 RGB views at known
orthographic camera poses; those drop straight into our baker, so the
non-commercial bake code is never needed.

INFERENCE INVOCATION (mirrored in inference.py::_generate_texture_mvpainter):
  pipe = MVPainter_Pipeline.from_pretrained('shaomq/MVPainter', torch_dtype=fp16)
  cn   = ControlNetModel_Union.from_unet(pipe.unet)
  pipe.add_controlnet(cn, conditioning_scale=1.0)
  ckpt = torch.load(hf_hub_download('shaomq/MVPainter',
                    'unet_w_controlnet/v29_25000.ckpt'),
                    map_location='cpu')['state_dict']   # CPU-map (issue #13 VRAM)
  pipe.unet.load_state_dict({k[5:]: v for k,v in ckpt.items() if 'unet' in k})
  out = pipe(ref_image, depth_image=normal_tile, depth_image_2=depth_tile,
             num_inference_steps=75)
  # out[0] = 3x2 grid of 6 RGB views (512*2 wide x 512*3 tall) → our baker.
